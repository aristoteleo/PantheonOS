The finalized 1000‑gene immune‑oncology panel provides balanced coverage of the tumor microenvironment, spanning major lineages and programs needed for robust cell state resolution. T/NK compartments are captured with TCR/CD3, naïve/activation (IL7R, CCR7, ICOS), cytotoxicity (NKG7, PRF1, GZMB), and exhaustion/checkpoint genes (PDCD1, CTLA4, LAG3, TIGIT, HAVCR2). B/plasma programs include MS4A1, CD79A/B, and plasma differentiation markers, while myeloid diversity is represented by monocyte/macrophage cores (LYZ, FCGR3A, LST1, C1Q), inflammatory S100A8/A9, dendritic modules (ITGAX, FCER1A/CLECs), and antigen presentation machinery (HLA class I/II, B2M, TAP1/2, CIITA). Cytokines/chemokines and their receptors (e.g., CXCL9/10/13, CCL2/5, CXCR3/4/5, CCR5/7) support mapping of trafficking, Th polarization, and interferon response. Stromal/endothelial/CAF and epithelial compartments are covered by collagen/ECM, perivascular, angiogenic, and epithelial markers, enabling cell–cell interaction context. Cancer signaling content captures proliferation (MKI67/TOP2A), EMT/stress/hypoxia/angiogenesis axes and hallmark oncogenic pathways, aiding discrimination of malignant versus non‑malignant epithelium and malignant state stratification.

Benchmarking indicates the panel preserves reference clustering structure: ARI/NMI are consistently high and competitive with HVG/DE/RF baselines, and silhouette scores support good separation of major TME lineages. UMAP similarity to full‑transcriptome embeddings is strong, showing near‑identical positioning of dominant immune, stromal, and tumor compartments. Strengths include robust resolution of T/NK activation–exhaustion gradients, macrophage polarization, plasma differentiation, and clear malignant–immune partitioning. Limitations are subtle and expected for a compact panel: slightly reduced separation among closely related myeloid/DC subsets and among T cell memory phenotypes in highly inflamed contexts, and potential under‑representation of rare or tissue‑specific states. Overall, the panel delivers broad, mechanism‑anchored TME and cancer signaling coverage with benchmarking that supports reliable cross‑study annotation and malignant state discrimination.
