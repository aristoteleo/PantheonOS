- FCRL2: Fc receptor–like immunoregulatory receptor expressed in B-cell subsets; associated with memory/mature B cells. https://www.genecards.org/cgi-bin/carddisp.pl?gene=FCRL2

- BLNK: B-cell linker adaptor critical for BCR signal transduction and B-cell development. https://www.genecards.org/cgi-bin/carddisp.pl?gene=BLNK
