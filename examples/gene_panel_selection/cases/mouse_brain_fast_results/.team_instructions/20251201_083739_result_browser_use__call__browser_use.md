Here is a concise, citable evidence set for each mouse CNS gene-to-cell-type/function claim. Each item lists 1–2 authoritative sources and a one-line summary with mouse context.

- Slc17a7 (VGLUT1): canonical excitatory glutamatergic neuron/synapse marker; VGLUT1 and VGLUT2 label complementary excitatory populations in adult brain (mouse data in Neuron 2001; review).
  - Neuron 2001: https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(01)00344-0
  - Review (Nature Reviews Neuroscience 2011): https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn2969

- Slc1a2 (GLT-1/EAAT2): astrocyte-enriched glutamate transporter; GLT-1 knockout mice develop lethal seizures, establishing its dominant role in glutamate uptake.
  - Science 1997: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.276.5319.1699
  - Danbolt review (Prog Neurobiol 2001): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11369436/

- Rbfox3 (NeuN): pan-neuronal nuclear marker; originally identified as a neuron-specific nuclear antigen and later shown to be RBFOX3.
  - Development 1992: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1483388/
  - J Biol Chem 2009: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19713214/

- P2ry12: microglia-enriched purinergic receptor; required for rapid microglial process extension toward injury in living mice and part of the homeostatic microglia signature.
  - Nat Neurosci 2006: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn1805
  - Nat Neurosci 2014: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.3599

- Cx3cr1: microglia/macrophage chemokine receptor; Cx3cr1-GFP knock-in mice label microglia/mononuclear phagocytes in CNS.
  - Mol Cell Biol 2000 (open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC85780/

- Pdgfra: OPC marker; PDGFRA+/NG2+ glia in adult mouse CNS generate myelinating oligodendrocytes (fate mapping/lineage tracing).
  - Nat Neurosci 2008: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.2220
  - J Neurosci 2010 (open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3063541/

- Gpr17: OPC/oligodendrocyte lineage marker; oligodendrocyte-lineage expression acts as a cell-intrinsic timer restraining maturation/myelination in mice.
  - Nat Neurosci 2009: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.2410
  - Review (J Biol Chem 2016, open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4705391/

- Tek (Tie2): endothelial receptor tyrosine kinase; Tie2/TEK is expressed almost exclusively in endothelial cells and controls angiopoietin signaling in vascular development (mouse).
  - NCBI Gene (mouse): https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/21687
  - Review (open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4211299/

- Pdgfrb: pericyte marker; PDGFRβ signaling in pericytes is essential for BBB formation and pericyte coverage in mouse brain.
  - Nature 2010: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature09522
  - Mol Neurodegener 2010 (open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2936891/

- Gabra6: cerebellar granule neuron-enriched GABA-A subunit; α6 subunit expression marks maturation of mouse cerebellar granule cells.
  - J Neurosci 1998: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/18/8/2822
  - NCBI Gene: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/14399

- Grid2: cerebellar Purkinje receptor (GluD2); selectively expressed in Purkinje cells and required for normal synapse function and motor coordination in mice.
  - Cell 1995: https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/0092-8674(95)90334-8
  - OMIM summary: https://www.omim.org/entry/602368

- Drd1 and Drd2: define direct/indirect pathway striatal MSNs; D1 in striatonigral (direct) and D2 in striatopallidal (indirect) projection neurons (foundational molecular mapping).
  - Science 1990: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.2147780

- Gpr88: striatal MSN-enriched orphan GPCR; highly expressed in striatum/MSNs, modulates striatal GPCR signaling and behavior in mouse models.
  - eLife 2020 (open): https://elifesciences.org/articles/50519
  - J Neurosci 2017: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/37/33/7939

- Htr2a: cortex-enriched (layers II/III/V) serotonergic receptor; EGFP-reporter mice show strong laminar cortical expression (notably layer V; layers II/III interneurons also implicated).
  - Frontiers Neurosci 2010 (open): https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2010.00036/full

- Kcnc1 (Kv3.1): fast-spiking parvalbumin interneuron marker; Kv3.1b co-expressed with PV in cortical/hippocampal GABAergic fast-spiking interneurons enabling high-frequency firing.
  - J Neurosci 1996 (PDF): https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/16/2/506.full.pdf

- Ednra: vascular smooth muscle/pericyte endothelin receptor; ETA (EDNRA) is the vasoconstrictor endothelin receptor in vascular smooth muscle, and brain capillary pericytes constrict to endothelin-1 in situ (mouse).
  - Mechanistic review (open): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9300638/
  - Nature 2006 (pericyte ET-1 constriction): https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17036005/

- Kcnj10 (Kir4.1): astrocyte potassium channel; astrocyte-specific Kir4.1 deletion in mice depolarizes glia, impairs K+/glutamate uptake, and causes seizures/ataxia.
  - J Neurosci 2007: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/27/42/11354

- Kcnj8/Kcnj11 (Kir6.1/6.2): vascular smooth muscle/endothelial KATP channels; Kir6.1 (Kcnj8) underlies vascular smooth muscle KATP controlling reactivity/BP in mice; classic reviews summarize vascular KATP composition/function (including Kir6.x/SUR).
  - Nat Med 2002: https://www.nature.com/articles/nm0502-466
  - Physiol Rev 1997 (PDF): https://journals.physiology.org/doi/epdf/10.1152/physrev.1997.77.4.1165
  - Open-access review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1779997/
