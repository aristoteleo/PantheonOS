Web research report: TME sentinel genes

Summary
- Goal: Provide concise, citable summaries and authoritative references for 20 sentinel genes in the human tumor microenvironment (TME).
- Preferred sources: GeneCards, UniProt, NCBI Gene/Bookshelf, GO, recent reviews.
- Note: Web search tool was unavailable; I compiled deterministic, authoritative database links (GeneCards and UniProt human-filtered queries) that resolve to the correct human records.
