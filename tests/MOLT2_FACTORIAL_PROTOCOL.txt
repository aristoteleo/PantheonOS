Development-only Molt2 factorial prototype

Base: 85d221d664be3d830b11b14be552ed68733f57e6. Vanilla methods and v1 source unchanged.
CLI: python examples/evolution_bench/run_bench.py --task TASK --method molt2_factorial --seed SEED --workers 2 --max-eval-calls 80 [ordinary model and budget flags]
No paid model calls or task evaluations were launched to construct this prototype.

One model template, four normal driver evaluations in fixed order00,10,01,11.
Two real module/file-scope flags produce immutable source states; all other bytes
must match. Python names cannot be reassigned or shadowed. C++ declarations must
be canonical static constexpr bool at file scope and cannot be shadowed by normal
bool/int/auto declarations. Flag names in preprocessing directives are rejected,
including line-spliced uses; normal C++ conditionals are required. Compiler validation remains necessary; this lexical
checker is not a proof of program semantics, mechanism independence or flag reachability.

Recorded conditional contrasts: A=y10-y00, B=y01-y00,
interaction=y11-y10-y01+y00. Missing/invalid/nonfinite/full-timeout values are never
zero-imputed. All effects are withheld if full source contrast fails. Anchor00
agreement is separately diagnostic; confounded effects remain labelled, not support.
No evaluator labels, hidden inputs or heldout scores are accessed by the method.

All observed executable source states, full SHA256s, IDs, context fingerprint,
score/error metadata and complete multiline mechanism comments are checkpointed.
The incremental evidence prompt projection is capped at24000characters AND UTF-8
bytes. Long annotations/errors are explicitly truncated with original SHA256,
character/byte lengths; newest complete projected records are retained and omitted
records counted. Numeric outcomes and source/context hashes within retained records
are preserved. This is NOT a ceiling on the entire prompt: native parent code still
uses the original prompting path. Complete raw evidence/source state stays intact.
Ordinary mutations receive code-linked observations (including negative and
inconclusive states), rather than an LLM surrogate's confidence or universal law.
A competitive usable arm can enter its original protected chain even if the
mechanism explanation is confounded. Fitness credit and evidential support differ.

Two original chains remain protected; at most one experiment open. Default
experiment fraction.35 applies to reserved arm evaluation slots: four per factorial
versus ordinary k=2, including unsuccessful generation reservations. Warmup6usable
measurements and interval3base batches remain. CLI max-eval-calls conservatively
reserves seed+all dispatched arms, including pending work; near cap it falls back
to ordinary2/1arm batches rather than launching incomplete factorial work.
Stopped/resumed pending experiments are archived interrupted_unresolved, with
partial executable states preserved, no invented outcomes and no automatic retries.

Offline validation: tests/test_molt2.py plus tests/test_molt2_factorial.py.
Potential gains remain hypotheses. Fresh development can inform a frozen variant;
confirmation/heldout outcomes must not be used for further tuning or broad superiority claims.
