# PantheonOS Roadmap

This roadmap describes the direction of PantheonOS. It is a living document;
priorities may shift with community input. Detailed work is tracked in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/aristoteleo/PantheonOS/issues) and
[Discussions](https://github.com/aristoteleo/PantheonOS/discussions).

## Vision

Make the open-source life-science software ecosystem AI-native: composable by
agents, improvable by agents, collaborative for teams, and usable on data that
cannot leave the institution that holds it.

## Themes

### 1. A framework for building agentic platforms

Turn Pantheon from a library that developers assemble by hand into a foundation
that others build on. Agent-driven composition of self-deployable,
domain-specific agentic platforms from Pantheon components, with authentication,
user management, and multi-tenancy included.

### 2. Multi-user collaboration

Self-hosted, multi-user workspaces so a whole lab can collaborate around shared
analyses, with roles and permissions.

### 3. Agentic evolution of scientific code

A standalone, self-serve harness that lets maintainers improve their own
open-source algorithms with agents; a public benchmark for evaluating agentic
evolution of bioinformatics code; and agent-assisted language migration between
ecosystems (for example, R to Python).

### 4. Foundation-model optimization

Apply agentic evolutionary tuning to the downstream-task performance of
foundation models, including the emerging virtual-cell and virtual-embryo model
classes.

### 5. Privacy-preserving and distributed execution

Decouple agents from execution so sensitive data can be analysed in place;
automatically audit agent-generated code for privacy risks; and support
federated training of foundation models without centralizing data.

### Ongoing

Documentation and tutorials, the Pantheon Store of installable skills, agents,
and teams, stability and performance, and growing the contributor community and
its governance.

## How to influence the roadmap

Open a [Discussion](https://github.com/aristoteleo/PantheonOS/discussions) or an
issue. Community proposals are welcome and shape what we build next.
