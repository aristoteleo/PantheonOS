# PantheonOS Governance

## Overview

PantheonOS is an open-source project (BSD-2-Clause) started and currently led by
the Qiu Lab at Stanford University. This document describes how decisions are
made and how people take on responsibility in the project. It is intended to
evolve as the community grows.

## Roles

### Users

Anyone who uses PantheonOS. Users contribute by reporting bugs, requesting
features, asking and answering questions, and participating in
[Discussions](https://github.com/aristoteleo/PantheonOS/discussions).

### Contributors

Anyone who contributes code, documentation, skills, tests, reviews, or community
support. Contributions are made via pull requests and are subject to review.
Contributors agree to the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and license their
contributions under BSD-2-Clause. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Maintainers

Contributors with a sustained track record who have been granted merge rights.
Maintainers review and merge pull requests, triage issues, guide the roadmap,
and mentor new contributors. New maintainers are nominated by an existing
maintainer and confirmed by consensus of the current maintainers, based on the
quality and consistency of their contributions.

### Lead maintainers

The lead maintainers set overall technical direction, are responsible for
releases, and resolve disputes that cannot be settled by consensus. The current
lead maintainers are:

- Weize Xu (core maintainer, first author) — wzxu@stanford.edu
- Xiaojie Qiu (Qiu Lab, Stanford University) — xiaojie@stanford.edu

## Decision-making

Most decisions are made by lazy consensus: a proposal raised in an issue,
Discussion, or pull request that receives no sustained objection within a
reasonable period is accepted. Substantial or contentious changes should be
raised in an issue or Discussion first.

Code changes require review and approval by at least one maintainer other than
the author before merging. When consensus cannot be reached, the lead
maintainers make the final decision.

## Agent-authored contributions

PantheonOS is a self-evolving system: some contributions are generated by
Pantheon's own agents. These follow the same governance as human contributions,
and additionally require review and approval by a **named human maintainer**
before merging. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Releases

Releases are published to [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pantheon-agents) and
GitHub Releases by the maintainers. The project follows semantic versioning
where practical.

## Changing this document

Changes to governance are proposed via pull request and adopted by consensus of
the maintainers.
